AI Assist — Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

AI Assist is a private voice assistant running on a device owned by one household. This policy explains how it handles data from Google accounts linked to it.

Data accessed

  • Your email address (to show which account is linked).
  • The list of your Google calendars (scope calendar.calendarlist.readonly).
  • Events in the calendars you selected for sync (scope calendar.events).

How the data is used

The data is used only to keep your tasks and reminders in the assistant in sync with your Google Calendar, and to tell you about changes to them. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens and synced events are stored only on the household's own device. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by AI Assist.
  • The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone outside the household.
  • Calendar data is not used to train AI models.

AI Assist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in your Google account under Third-party apps & services. On request, the device owner deletes the stored tokens and synced data.
